THE PROVENANCE

Born from an Obsession with Rest

Most modern bedding relies on chemical softeners—silicone baths designed to wash out after two cycles, leaving stiff, abrasive threads against your skin. We took the unhurried path.

Our journey begins in the maritime soil of Normandy, where cool sea breezes nourish the finest flax fibers on earth. Woven by multigenerational artisans in Northern France, each roll of linen is stone-washed with smooth volcanic pumice to unlock instant softness while preserving the tensile strength of the fiber.
